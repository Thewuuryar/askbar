# Alerts

Everything that needs your attention, most serious first, in up to three groups by severity: critical, warning and info. The bell in the status bar and the Needs attention list on [Home](home.md) are made from the same alerts. The Notification settings button opens the page where you choose how alerts reach you: [Notifications](settings/notifications.md). Each kind of alert, with its severity, is listed in [Alert kinds](audit/alert-kinds.md).

## Critical

Something is wrong now and needs action, such as the audit disk being nearly full, a part of Askbar having gone quiet, or a backup that failed or is missed. The group is shown in red and leads the page.

## Warning

Something changed or needs a look, but nothing has stopped: for example sealed audit content was opened, the retention or the backup key was changed, or a service restarted repeatedly.

## Info

For your information only, such as a newer release being available or a test alert. You can dismiss an info alert; the dismissal is kept in this browser only.
